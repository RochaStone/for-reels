# Cheeky Charm

Chatty, fast and funny: a numbered list. A cheeky one-sentence hook in yellow condensed serif with a thin black
outline sits on her chest for the first 3.4 seconds, once. Bold white caption chunks run lower down. Each list
point gets one image cut in on her chest, left of centre. No number cards: the numbers live in her voice. Props in
hand do the rest. Everything hard-cuts.

## Type
- Display: Instrument Serif 400, sentence case: the hook.
- Text and captions: Montserrat 800, sentence case, punctuation dropped.
- Mono: Space Mono 400.

## Colour
- Yellow #F9FE19 hook (accent) with a black outline. White #FFFFFF captions with a dark #222222 edge. Green
  #3DDC84 gains, red #FF4D4D losses. Grounds #FFFFFF and #111111, square corners.

## Captions
Chunks of 4 to 7 words, 50px, centred on y 1385, max width 680, swapped whole about every 2.1s, hard cut, no
karaoke. No box: the engine's soft shadow stands in for the reference's thin dark edge.

## Motion
Normal. No animation at all: hard cuts, jump cuts every 3 to 4s with framing changes, a big lean-in close-up at
the end. Grade: none.

## Sound
Light: a soft pop on each image.

## Hi-fi
A one-line hook: the yellow outlined sentence on her chest from frame 0. Captions as whole lines. No emphasis
devices, nothing behind the head, cards rarely. A still camera that alternates framings on joins. Short J-lead.

## Lo-fi
The whole chunk lands at once, 6 words or 34 characters. No text boxes. One image per point on top, often. No
backdrops, no zooms.

## Showcase
The emphatic word slams in whole. Hard cuts only. An effect on most beats. The motif is the list image.

## Never
- Number cards, a second headline, animation on type, anything right of x 900 or below y 1520, her face covered.

## Template pieces
1. **cheeky-charm-hook** (top). Her hook as one sentence (written or spoken). `--font-display` 86px,
   `--c-accent`, a 3px #000000 outline (the template's signature; the kit's no-outline rule is set aside for this
   piece), no shadow, no box, centred on x 540 and y 1030, max width 700, line-height 1.0, balanced lines of 3 to 4
   words (about 4 lines). On from frame 0, hard off at 3.4s. Shown once.
2. **cheeky-charm-list-image** (top). One image per list point, native aspect, 520px wide (max 440 tall), left
   edge x 170, bottom edge y 1320, square corners, no border or shadow (images on white keep their white). Hard cut
   in about 3.3s after "number X", on the noun it shows; held about 3.8s (2.8 to 5.7s); out on the next jump cut or
   the next number.
3. **cheeky-charm-follow-card** (over, optional, not in the source build). The last 0.6s: a hard punch-in to a
   close-up with her own closing words as the caption.
